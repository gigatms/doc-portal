本文件入口目前提供 Phase 1 的 EU DoC 公開目錄與下載，涵蓋適用的 RED、EMC、LVD 及 RoHS。自 2027 年 12 月 11 日起投放歐盟市場的產品，其 CRA 符合性將依 CRA 第 28(3) 條併入該型號的單一 EU DoC。

The portal currently provides the Phase 1 EU DoC catalogue and downloads, covering applicable RED, EMC, LVD and RoHS legislation. For products placed on the EU market from 11 December 2027, CRA conformity will be included in each applicable model’s single EU DoC in accordance with CRA Article 28(3).

CRA Article 14：通報義務自 2026 年 9 月 11 日起已適用；請參閱 security.gigatms.com.tw。

CRA Article 14: The reporting obligation applies from 11 September 2026. See security.gigatms.com.tw.
