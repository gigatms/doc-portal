ADVISORY FIELD GUIDE · 欄位說明

每一筆公告都具備可追溯欄位

欄位定義對應內部公告範本與審核要求。Field definitions follow the controlled advisory template and review requirements.

公告編號 · Advisory ID

格式：GTMS-SA-YYYY-###

CVE

已申請則填入；未申請時顯示「—」。Use “—” when no CVE has been assigned.

嚴重度 · Severity

標示 CVSS v4.0 分數與等級，例如 8.1 High。

狀態 · Status

已發布 · Published／已更新 · Updated／已撤回 · Withdrawn

首次發布日 · First Published

公告首次公開的日期；修訂時不變。The date the advisory was first published; it does not change when revised.

最後更新日 · Last Updated

公告內容最近一次更新的日期。The date the advisory content was most recently updated.

已超過支援期的產品 · Products beyond support life

若漏洞影響已超過支援期之產品，公告中會說明，並提供緩解或汰換建議。

If a vulnerability affects a product beyond its support life, the advisory will say so and provide mitigation or replacement guidance.

公開範圍界線 · Publication boundary

技術檔案、SBOM、未修正漏洞細節、內部風險評估與 OEM 機密資料不會放在公開安全公告頁。

Technical files, SBOMs, unremediated vulnerability details, internal risk assessments and OEM confidential information are not published here.

漏洞通報 · Report a vulnerability

如要通報產品安全問題，請前往 GIGA-TMS 資安通報網站，依該站公告的通報流程提交資訊。

To report a product security issue, visit the GIGA-TMS security reporting site and follow its reporting process.
