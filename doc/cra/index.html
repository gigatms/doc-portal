CRA INFORMATION

CRA requirements.
Included in each EU DoC.

自 2027 年 12 月 11 日起，適用 CRA 的產品，其 CRA 符合性將依 CRA 第 28(3) 條併入該型號的單一 EU DoC。From 11 December 2027, where the CRA applies to a product, its CRA conformity will be included in that model’s single EU DoC in accordance with Article 28(3).

Single EU DoC: Where a product is subject to more than one Union legal act requiring an EU declaration of conformity, CRA Article 28(3) provides for one EU declaration covering all such acts.

CRA 第 28(3) 條規定，產品如受多項要求 EU 符合性聲明的歐盟法規規範，應以一份 EU DoC 涵蓋所有相關法規。查看 EUR-Lex 法規原文 ↗

EU DECLARATION OF CONFORMITY

One model.
One applicable declaration.

For products placed on the EU market from 11 December 2027, the applicable CRA conformity information will be incorporated into the relevant model’s single EU DoC. This portal will not publish a separate CRA declaration catalogue.

自 2027 年 12 月 11 日起投放歐盟市場的產品，適用的 CRA 符合性資訊將併入該型號的單一 EU DoC。本入口不另行建立獨立的 CRA 符合性聲明目錄。
